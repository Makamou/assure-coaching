Why it matters

Separate what you’ve learned to do from what you’re made to do.

Your innate gifts are the abilities and ways of being that tend to energize you, influence how you naturally contribute, and shape the problems, people and possibilities that draw you in.

For many high-achieving professionals, years of meeting expectations make it hard to tell what you’ve learned to do well from what you’re naturally equipped and inspired to do. This assessment brings those patterns into view, giving you greater Clarity, Confidence & Courage as you consider what comes next.

There are no right or wrong answers. You aren’t compared with an ideal personality or profile. Your responses reveal a combination of strengths, motivations and preferences: a signature that is uniquely yours.

More than a career assessment

It isn’t a job-matching quiz. It’s a creation tool.

The assessment won’t tell you which job to pursue or place you into a predetermined career category. Instead of asking only “What job fits me?”, it helps you explore deeper questions.

  • Who am I beneath the roles I have learned to perform?
  • What comes naturally to me?
  • What am I most energized to contribute?
  • What human needs or problems do I care most about?
  • Where might my gifts create meaningful value?

Your results illuminate three dimensions:

Who

who you naturally are

How

how you naturally act and create

Why

why certain forms of contribution matter to you

You’ll also identify the human needs you’re most passionate about addressing, and the areas of work where you feel energized to make a difference.

Benefits

What you gain

  • Recognize natural strengths you may have overlooked or taken for granted.
  • Separate acquired professional skills from deeper innate gifts.
  • Understand what naturally energizes, motivates and inspires you.
  • Gain language for the unique value you bring to others.
  • Identify the needs and problems you feel drawn to address.
  • Explore where your gifts may find their most meaningful expression.
  • Approach reinvention with greater clarity, confidence and direction.
  • Begin connecting who you are with the work, service or creation that could become your Life’s Work.

This isn’t about becoming someone new. It’s about seeing who you already are.

…and discovering how more of that person can be expressed in the world. Welcome to your next level of self-discovery.
